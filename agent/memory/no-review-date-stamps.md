Do not write editorial review stamps into page copy: no "Reviewed 10 August 2026", no "Regulatory dates reviewed on...", no "at the time of writing, August 2026". Statutory and regulatory dates inside the content itself are the opposite of this and must stay, because they are the reason the section has value.

**Why:** a review stamp dates the whole page the moment it ships, reads as hedging to a buyer, and is a maintenance promise nobody goes back and keeps. The regulation's own dates age visibly on their own.

**How to apply:** when converting a `COPY-` file that carries a review stamp, strip it during the conversion rather than shipping it. Replace "as of August 2026 X is still true" with the plain assertion. Applies to every page type, not just industries. See [[zyneto-industry-pages]] and [[content-delivery-workflow]].
